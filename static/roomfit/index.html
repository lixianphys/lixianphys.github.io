This is a sample flat. Move things around to see how it works, then start a blank plan and enter your own measurements.

Drawing a room. Click each corner. Type a length in cm and press Enter to place the next corner exactly. Click the first corner to finish. Backspace undoes a corner.

Drawing an inner wall. Tap where it starts, then where it ends. For an exact length, type it after the first tap and use an arrow. Ends snap onto walls.

Drag to move · Shift+drag or empty space to pan · wheel to zoom · R or the knob to rotate ([ ] 15°, Alt no snap) · Del remove · all sizes in cm
